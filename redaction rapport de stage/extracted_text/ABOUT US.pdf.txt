===== PAGE 1 =====
Flamenco Agency is a platform that promotes artists, shows and
knowledge on the art of flamenco at an international level. It presents large
ballets, small groups and artists (of both traditional and avant-garde
aesthetics) in all disciplines: dance, singing and toque (playing), as well as
other styles such as the use of various instruments (piano, percussion, etc).
As a multidisciplinary agency, it organises worldwide tours for the most
renowned Spanish flamenco artists.  
Based in Seville, where Flamenco was born, Flamenco Agency was founded
in 2007 by Dr. Jaime Trancoso. Jaime graduated with a degree in
musicology from the Universidad Autónoma de Madrid. He received his PhD
in Flamenco from the University of Seville (obtaining a prize by the Seville
City Council for his doctoral thesis) and became a cultural manager after
finishing his studies at the University of London. 
Thanks to his qualified research training in flamenco and his extensive
professional experience through his continuous work with artists and
productions in Spain, Flamenco Agency offers thorough advice as well as
the highest expert knowledge in each of its artistic proposals for festivals,
theatres, and opera houses around the world. 
 


===== PAGE 2 =====
The vision of Flamenco Agency is to
elevate and place Flamenco within the
highest category of the performing arts. In
fact, in its more than fifteen years of
experience, it has given value to this
artistic and popular discipline that
constitutes to the most representative
identity of the Andalusian and Spanish
culture. Apart from that, Flamenco Agency
presents proposals of the highest artistic
quality to international stages. These
proposals are based on the knowledge of
the flamenco shows that are produced and
premiered in Spain, both in the Flamenco
Art Biennial of Sevilla and in the Jerez
Flamenco Festival. 
We are specialised in the distribution of the
best flamenco ballets in Spain, and
consequently, in the world, such as the
Andalusian Flamenco Ballet (official ballet of
the Andalusian government) or the Barcelona
Flamenco Ballet. Additionally, Flamenco
Agency promotes both traditional shows
(such as Carmen or Flamenco Suite), as well
as avant-garde and contemporary flamenco
art, bailaores (dancers), cantaores (singers),
guitarists and pianists. Moreover, thanks to
the flexibility and adaptability of Flamenco
Agency's tailor-made proposals, we have also
organised corporate event shows for
companies such as Hewlett-Packard and for
the Royal House of Spain.


===== PAGE 3 =====
Flamenco Agency has been programmed in important festivals
around the world, such as the Bangkok's International Festival of
Music & Dance, Gent Festival of Flanders, Ljubljana Festival, Ohrid
Summer Festival, Smetana’s Litomyšl or Jeonju Int’l Sori Festival.
Similarly, it has collaborated with some of the most prestigious
venues and opera houses, such as the National Center for the
Performing Arts Opera House in Beijing (NCPA), Shanghai
International Dance Center Grand Theatre (SIDC), Aga Khan
Museum (Canada), or the Teatro Campoamor de Oviedo (Spain).  
In addition, Jaime Trancoso selflessly offers conferences, courses,
masterclasses, or didactic seminars, even with pre-concert
formats. So much so he has participated in many international
conferences, such as the Royal Opera House Muscat in Oman
(ROHM), Førde Traditional and World Music Festival (Norway, EFFE
Award winners) or Sibiu International Theatre Festival (FITS,
Romania).  


===== PAGE 4 =====
As a result of its commitment to the performing arts, Flamenco Agency
maintains a consortium and concerted actions with the International
Society for the Performing Arts (ISPA). Simultaneously, it forms part of the
business circle of the Association of Asia Pacific Performing Arts Centres
(AAPPAC) and has collaborated closely with the most prestigious festivals
of the European Festivals Association (EFA). 
Finally, Flamenco Agency has been participating annually at the most
prestigious conferences for the performing arts such as Arts Festivals
Summit (EFA), APAP, China SPAF, CINARS, Classical Next, Exib Música, IETM,
Mapas Mercado Cultural, Opera Europa, PAMS, Sibiu Performing Arts
Market, Tanzmesse or WOMEX.
